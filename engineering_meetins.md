## Product Team

### Weekly Planning [45 min]

Agenda:

- 13:00 - 13:10 - What we have done last cycle/week:
  - Go through DONE tasks (_1+ sentences by the team member: why+what_)
  - Move all DONE tasks to COMPLETED.
  - You can ask questions about the completed tasks if something is unclear.

- 13:10 - 13:20 - Context/Goal for the next cycle:
  - Any changes to the current goals? (Product Leader)
  - Any new learnings? (Product Leader, team members)
  - Highlights from demos, interactions with customers, competition (Product Leader)

- 13:20 - 13:40 - What we do next week:
  - Go through IN PROGRESS and TODO
  - Look at the top of the Backlog
  - Do we have enough work? Do we know what we will do?

- 13:40 - 13:45 Closing:
  - Any pending topics? Schedule additional meetings if needed.
  - Selecting the next moderator
