# Onboarding

A key to make new team members become productive, effective, and successful in their new teams.

## Elements

1. Onboarding buddy
2. (dedicated) Onboarding tasks at least 2
3. 7-30-60 plan
4. Topics that manager must discuss on their 1o1ns

## 1. Onboarding buddy

Responsibilities:

1. Onboarding is the top prority for the onboarding buddy.
2. Signal performance of new joiner both positive and things to correct.
3. Show - by example - culture and the way we work.

How often to meet:

1. [1st week] Meet every day at least one (in person/video call), or even two times (beginning and end of the day)
2. [2nd week] Every day
3. [3-5 weeks] every second -> once per week
