# Onboarding

A key to make new team members become productive, effective, and successful in their new teams.

## Elements

1. Onboarding buddy
2. (dedicated) Onboarding tasks at least 2
3. 7-30-60 plan
4. Topics that manager must discuss on their 1on1s

## 1. Onboarding buddy

Responsibilities:

1. Onboarding is the top priority for the onboarding buddy.
2. Signal performance of new joiner both positive and things to correct.
3. Show - by example - culture and the way we work.

How often to meet:

1. [1st week] Meet every day at least once (in person/video call), or even two times (beginning and end of the day)
2. [2nd week] Every day
3. [3-5 weeks] every second -> once per week

Notice - recognize the importance of the role:

- Write in the welcome message who is the new joiner's onboarding buddy.
- During perf review, talk about how well the new joiner - that the person onboarded - is doing.
- Check during 1on1 with the onboarding buddy, how the process goes and how the new person is doing.
